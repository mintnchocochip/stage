Errors block the build. Warnings ask for a second look.

  • Dash characters and spaced hyphens
  • Emoji on a slide
  • Default AI fonts
  • Numbers missing from the ledger
  • Quotes not found in their source
  • Generic titles like Overview
  • Stock phrases
  • Sentences over 24 words
  • Two slides in a row with one layout

Close on the guarantee. The build refuses to ship a deck that invents numbers.
